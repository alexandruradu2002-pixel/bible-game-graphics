# Credits — chapter 39, David and Goliath (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/david-and-goliath/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/david_*.py`) from the layers of the LPC
character generator, from the drawings of other chapters and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner. The drawings
made from code by the chapter (`capitole/david*.gd`: the smoke of the sacrifice, the darkness of the evil spirit, the
rings and the line of the sling, the flight of the stone with its shadow, the close-up harp with its strings, Saul's
chair and room in the harp game, the pasture in the memory with the thicket, the semicircle of the armour and the rim
of the helmet) are drawn by us, with nothing from the packs.

From other chapters, used by path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): Samuel, Saul, his servant and
the messenger (the kinsman) from Saul (`graphics/saul/`, `credits/saul.md`), the elders and the people from The Burning
Bush (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the soldiers of Israel and of the Philistines (the Israelites and the
Amalekites) and the young goat from The Wilderness (`graphics/the-wilderness/`, `credits/the-wilderness.md`), the calf, the houses, the
wall and the gate from Mamre (`graphics/mamre/`, `credits/mamre.md`; the coping of the wall along the sides of the
courtyard, the narrow strip at the top, is made by us from the top rows of the wall from Mamre, flipped vertically), the
cow, the goat, the bear and the altar from The Flood (`graphics/the-flood/`, `credits/the-flood.md`), the lamb and Abel (for the
pasture in the memory), the thorns and the branches from The Brothers (`graphics/the-brothers/`), the boulders of the ford from
The Call (the stepping stones on the stream in the valley, `credits/the-call.md`), and the rest of the things on the map
from The Threshing Floor, The Call, The Tabernacle, The Well, The Storehouses, Haran, Jacob, Joseph, Moriah, The
Generations, The Tower, The Plagues and Balaam and Nebo (each with its own credits file); the wild beasts and the ground,
as in the other chapters (`credits/wild-animals.md`, `credits/haran.md`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them under the same
terms.
