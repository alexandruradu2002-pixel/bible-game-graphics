# Credits — chapter 25, The Spies (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-spies/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/iscoadele_*.gd`) from the people sheets
of other chapters and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner. The drawings made from code by the chapter
(`capitole/iscoadele_desene.gd`: the grape cluster, the carrying pole, the level, the bronze serpent and the others) are drawn by us, with
nothing from the packs.

From other chapters, used by path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): Moses, Aaron, Miriam, the elders,
the people and the donkeys (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), Joshua and the elders (`graphics/sinai/`, `credits/sinai.md`),
the boy's family (`graphics/the-plagues/`, `credits/the-plagues.md`), the people, the young goat, the donkey, the Amalekites (the men of Edom), the rock, the spring, the bushes and the heap from The Wilderness
(`graphics/the-wilderness/`, `credits/the-wilderness.md`), the tabernacle, the cloud, the ark and the bellows (`graphics/the-tabernacle/`, `credits/the-tabernacle.md`); the tents, the oak, the trough, the mound, the jar, the Canaanites (`graphics/the-call/`), the bushes, the palm trees and the well from Haran (`graphics/haran/`), the boulders and the step from Moriah (`graphics/moriah/`), the mountain rocks from The Flood (`graphics/the-flood/`), the vine and the basket of figs from Joseph (`graphics/joseph/`), the wheat from The Plagues, the sheaf from The Basket, the wall, the gate and the houses from Mamre (`graphics/mamre/`), the low stone wall from The Brothers (`graphics/the-brothers/`), the furnace, the flames and the sitting stone from The Generations (`graphics/the-generations/`), each with its chapter's credits file.

**What the licences require:** images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them
under the same terms. The authors of the layers are those in the credits file of the chapter from which the base sheet is taken.
