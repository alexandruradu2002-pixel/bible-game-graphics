# Credits — the prologue (Haran) and the drawings shared with chapter 7

The drawings in `graphics/haran/`, used by the prologue and by chapter 7 (The Call). They are cut out, recoloured or composed
from LPC packs on OpenGameArt.org and from the LPC character generator; some are drawn by us, in the LPC manner
(dark outline, stepped shading, light from the top left). The authors of the packs must appear on the Credits screen,
as must those in `credits/shared.md`. The images made from CC-BY-SA material remain CC-BY-SA.
