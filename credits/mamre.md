# Credits — chapter 9, Mamre (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/mamre/` are composed from free LPC packs on OpenGameArt.org and from the LPC character
generator, and some are drawn by us, in the LPC manner (`unelte/lpc/mamvri.py`). The sounds in `sunete/mamvri/`
are synthesised by us (`unelte/sunete_mamvri.py`), with no third-party files.

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
screen. Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground, the people's layers, the dead tree) also remain
CC-BY-SA: anyone may reuse them under the same conditions.

## The sounds

All 29 of the chapter's sounds (the kneading, the sizzling of the unleavened cakes, the water, the skin bag, the cows and the calf,
Sarah's laughter, the city in the evening, the thunder, the falling fire, the running footsteps, the pillar of salt, the smoke) are made
by computation, for this game.
