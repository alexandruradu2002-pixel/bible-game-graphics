# Credits — chapter 36, The Threshing Floor (the "lpc" style)

Ruth, Naomi and Boaz are the sheets shared by chapters 35 and 36, from `graphics/ruth/` (their sources are in `credits/ruth.md`).
The images in `graphics/the-threshing-floor/` are made by the chapter's tool (`unelte/lpc/aria_foi.gd`): the people's pose sheets
from their base sheets, and the objects pixel by pixel. The drawings made from code by the chapter (`capitole/aria*.gd`: the traced
path, the breath circle, the moonbeam, the firelight, the family tree) are drawn by us, with nothing from the packs.

From other chapters, used by path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): the ground from Haran
(`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the people from The Burning Bush (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the man and the young man from
The Spies (`graphics/the-spies/`, `credits/the-spies.md`), Judah, the woman, the fields of grain and the donkey from The Storehouses
(`graphics/the-storehouses/`, `credits/the-storehouses.md`), the house, the villager, the jug and the basket from The Call (`graphics/the-call/`,
`credits/the-call.md`), the houses, the wall and the city gate from Mamre (`graphics/mamre/`), the well, the oil lamp and the wine
jug from Jacob (`graphics/jacob/`), the stone step from The Well (`graphics/the-well/`), the olive trees, the bushes, the rocks and
the boulders from Moriah (`graphics/moriah/`), the fences, the curtain, the water vessel, the bread and the bowl from The Plagues
(`graphics/the-plagues/`), the sheaf from Joseph (`graphics/joseph/`), the sheaf lying down from Jericho (`graphics/jericho/`), the fire from
Balaam and Nebo (`graphics/balaam-and-nebo/`), the child and the baby from The Basket (`graphics/the-basket/`), the measure from The Wilderness
(`graphics/the-wilderness/`), each with its chapter's credits file.

**What the licences require:** images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them
under the same terms. The authors of the layers are the ones in the credits file of the chapter the base sheet is taken from.
