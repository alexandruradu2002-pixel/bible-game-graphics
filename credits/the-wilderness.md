# Credits — chapter 22, The Wilderness ("lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-wilderness/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/pustia_*.py`) from free LPC packs
on OpenGameArt.org, from the layers of the LPC character generator and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner.

The chapter also uses, by path, drawings from other chapters (their sources are in their own credit files):
Moses the shepherd and Aaron (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the boy, the father, the mother, the neighbours and the people of Goshen
(`graphics/the-plagues/`, `credits/the-plagues.md`), the black tents and the stone seat (`graphics/the-generations/`,
`credits/the-generations.md`), the dead tree (`graphics/mamre/`, `credits/mamre.md`), the altar stones
(`graphics/the-call/`, `credits/the-call.md`), the boulders, the dry tufts and the brushwood (`graphics/the-brothers/`,
`credits/the-brothers.md`), the hearth (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. The images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground of the maps, the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may
reuse them under the same terms.
