# Credits — chapter 1 (The Garden), the new drawings

The drawings in `graphics/the-garden/`, added when chapter 1 became "the book of Adam" (22 Oct 2026). They are cut out and
rearranged from LPC packs on OpenGameArt.org (unelte/lpc/eden.py makes them). The authors of the packs must appear on the
Credits screen, like those in `credits/shared.md`. Images made from CC-BY-SA material remain CC-BY-SA.
The sheep, the rabbit, the dove and the lion are the earlier ones (`credits/shared.md`). The deer and the fox were
removed from the chapter on 23 Oct 2026, together with their drawings.

### Drawn by us, pixel by pixel (24 Oct 2026, `unelte/lpc/eden_galeata.py`)

| Our file | What it is | From LPC |
|---|---|---|

| `eden_galeata_jos.png` | The bucket set down, with the handle lying flat | Nothing; our own drawing |
| `eden_carte_buton.png` | Adam's book in miniature, the button in the top right | Nothing; our own drawing |

The footprints on the ground, the feathers, the shadow of the tree, the circle of quiet, Adam's book (the big one), the
stream of water and the wet earth are made from code (they are not images). The new sounds (`sunete/eden/eden_*.wav`, and
the pouring from the bucket and the bucket being set down, `eden_galeata_*.wav`) are synthesised by us (`unelte/sunete_eden.py`).
