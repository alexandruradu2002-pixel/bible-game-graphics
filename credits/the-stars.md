# Credits — chapter 8 (The Stars)

The drawings in `graphics/the-stars/`. The rest of the chapter's drawings belong to other chapters (the prologue, The Call, The Tower,
The Generations, Mamre, Joseph), with their authors in the credit files of those chapters. The ones taken from chapters that
are not yet in the store apps (The Burning Bush, The Wilderness, Balaam and Nebo, Samuel) are copied here unchanged (18 Nov 2026),
under another name, so that the app does not have to carry the whole packs of those chapters for them; their authors remain the
ones in the credits of the chapter they come from.
