# Credits — chapter 27, Balaam and Nebo (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/balaam-and-nebo/` are made by the chapter's tool (`unelte/lpc/valaam_desene.gd`) from the drawings of other
chapters of the game (which come from free LPC packs on OpenGameArt.org and from the layers of the LPC character
generator) and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner. The authors and licences of those drawings are in the credits
files of their chapters, listed below.

The chapter also uses, by their path, drawings from other chapters (their sources are in their credits files): the mud-brick
houses, the low wall, the black tents (`graphics/the-generations/`, `credits/the-generations.md`), the stable and the manger
(`graphics/the-plagues/`, `credits/the-plagues.md`), the haystack and the mountain rocks (`graphics/the-flood/`, `credits/the-flood.md`),
the jugs (`graphics/the-basket/`, `credits/the-basket.md`), the trough, the shepherds and Moses as a shepherd (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`,
`credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the reeds and the altar (`graphics/the-call/`, `credits/the-call.md`), the courtyard gate and the city
wall (`graphics/mamre/`, `credits/mamre.md`), the vine stock (`graphics/joseph/`, `credits/joseph.md`), the dry-stone
walls and the rocks (`graphics/the-brothers/`, `credits/the-brothers.md`), the boulders, the bundle and the stack of firewood, the ram
(`graphics/moriah/`, `credits/moriah.md`), the palm trees (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the Tent of Meeting and the smoke
(`graphics/the-tabernacle/`, `credits/the-tabernacle.md`), the people of Israel, the elders, the loaded donkey and Moses with his arms raised
(`graphics/the-wilderness/`, `credits/the-wilderness.md`), an elder from Sinai (`graphics/sinai/`, `credits/sinai.md`), the camel
(`graphics/the-well/`, `credits/the-well.md`), the calf (`graphics/mamre/`), the sheep (`graphics/`, `credits/shared.md`), Joshua and
Eleazar (`graphics/the-spies/`, `credits/the-spies.md`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits page (they are
in the credits files named above). Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people, the donkey, the angel) remain
CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them under the same conditions.
