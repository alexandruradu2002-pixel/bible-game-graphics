## The sounds
All the sounds of the chapter (the axe, the tree, the saw, the pitch, the rain, the thunder, the waves, the creaking of the ark,
the dove, the beating of wings, the splashes, the raven, the animals, the pens on the decks, the fire of the sacrifice, the rainbow) are synthesised for this game (`unelte/sunete_potop.py`).
