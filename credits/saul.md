# Credits — chapter 38, Saul (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/saul/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/saul_*.py`) from the layers of the LPC character
generator, from the drawings of other chapters and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner. The drawings made from code by
the chapter (`capitole/saul*.gd`: the smoke of the sacrifice, the rain, the flame, the valley mist in "Two by Two", the stone step,
the full-screen rocks, the honeycomb with the bees, the branches and Jonathan's head at the honey, the signs on the road, the
outlines and the sight cones) are drawn by us, with nothing from the packs.

From other chapters, loaded by their path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): the people and the elders from
The Burning Bush (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the Israelites, the Amalekites (the Philistines), the man, the young goat
and the basket from The Wilderness (`graphics/the-wilderness/`, `credits/the-wilderness.md`), the priest from Jericho and the wet stones of the
Jordan (`graphics/jericho/`, `credits/jericho.md`), the houses, the wall and the gate from Mamre (`graphics/mamre/`,
`credits/mamre.md`), the villager, the house, the folded tent, the mound, the sack, the oak and the altar from The Call
(`graphics/the-call/`, `credits/the-call.md`), the grain fields, the shade shelter, the donkey and the ox from The Storehouses
(`graphics/the-storehouses/`, `credits/the-storehouses.md`), the long table, the mat, the stack of planks and the vessel of anointing oil from
The Tabernacle (`graphics/the-tabernacle/`, `credits/the-tabernacle.md`), the wicket gate and the shed from The Threshing Floor (`graphics/the-threshing-floor/`,
`credits/the-threshing-floor.md`), the well, the chest and the pillar from Jacob (`graphics/jacob/`, `credits/jacob.md`), the step and the pitcher
from The Well (`graphics/the-well/`, `credits/the-well.md`), the thorns and the branches from The Brothers (`graphics/the-brothers/`), the
flat stone and the hearth from Haran (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the sheaf and the vine from Joseph (`graphics/joseph/`,
`credits/joseph.md`), the olive tree, the bushes, the rocks and the boulders from Moriah (`graphics/moriah/`, `credits/moriah.md`), the
black tent, the field, the plough and the hearth with firewood from The Generations (`graphics/the-generations/`, `credits/the-generations.md`), the
mound, the large stone and the pillar from Peniel (`graphics/peniel/`, `credits/peniel.md`), the altar, the goat, the cow and the
fox from The Flood (`graphics/the-flood/`, `credits/the-flood.md`), the black tent from The Tower (`graphics/the-tower/`, `credits/the-tower.md`), the
