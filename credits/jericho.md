# Credits — chapters 28 and 29, Rahab and Jericho ("lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/jericho/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/ierihonul_sol.py`,
`unelte/lpc/ierihonul_desene.gd`, `unelte/lpc/ierihonul_funia.gd`) from the LPC terrain pieces, from the drawings of other
chapters and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner.

From other chapters, taken by path (their authors are in the credits file of their chapter): grey-haired Joshua and Eleazar in
Aaron's vestments (`graphics/the-spies/`, `credits/the-spies.md`), the tents, the reeds, the waterskin, the basket, the jug,
the goat (`graphics/the-call/`), the acacias, the palm trees, the bushes, the hearth (`graphics/haran/`), the Tent of Meeting, the courtyard, the cloud,
the ark (`graphics/the-tabernacle/`), the Israelites (`graphics/the-wilderness/`, `graphics/the-plagues/`, `graphics/the-basket/`,
`graphics/sinai/`), the watchmen and the people of Jericho (`graphics/joseph/`, `graphics/the-call/`, `graphics/the-well/`),
the spies (`graphics/joseph/`, `graphics/the-generations/`), the Commander (`graphics/jacob/`), the children (`graphics/peniel/`), the bread,
the figs, the sheaves, the sack (`graphics/the-plagues/`, `graphics/joseph/`, `graphics/mamre/`, `graphics/the-storehouses/`), the well
(`graphics/the-well/`), the donkey (`graphics/the-wilderness/`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. The images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground, the people) remain CC-BY-SA: anyone may reuse them under the
same terms.
