# Credits — chapter 23, Sinai (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/sinai/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/sinai_*.py`) from free LPC packs on
OpenGameArt.org, from the layers of the LPC character generator, from the drawings of other chapters and from drawings
made by us, in the LPC manner.

From other chapters, used by path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): Moses and Aaron
(`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the families in the camp (`graphics/the-plagues/`, `credits/the-plagues.md`, and
`graphics/the-basket/`, `credits/the-basket.md`), the tents and the altar (`graphics/the-call/`,
`credits/the-call.md`), the palm trees and the bushes (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the boulders (`graphics/moriah/`),
the rocks (`graphics/the-flood/`), the hand mill (`graphics/the-basket/`), the white clouds (`graphics/the-tower/`, `credits/the-tower.md`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground of the maps, the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may
reuse them under the same terms.
