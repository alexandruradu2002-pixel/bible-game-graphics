# Credits — chapter 6, The Tower ("lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-tower/` are made by the chapter's tools from free LPC packs on OpenGameArt.org,
from the layers of the LPC character generator and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner.

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. The images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground of the plain, the reeds, the firewood, the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0:
anyone may reuse them under the same terms.
