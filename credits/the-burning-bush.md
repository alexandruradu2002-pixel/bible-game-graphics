# Credits — chapter 18, The Burning Bush (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-burning-bush/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/rugul_*.py`, with the tools of The Basket)
from the layers of the LPC character generator, from the donkey of The Basket and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner.
Midian and the wilderness use the ground and the objects of the prologue (`graphics/haran/`, see `credits/haran.md`), and
the brickyard and the town quarter use those of The Basket (`graphics/the-basket/`, see `credits/the-basket.md`); the sheep are the
game's sheep (`graphics/oaie.png`, see `credits/shared.md`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people, the donkeys) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them under the
same conditions.
