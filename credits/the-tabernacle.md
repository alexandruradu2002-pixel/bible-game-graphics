# Credits — chapter 24, The Tabernacle (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-tabernacle/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/cortul_*.py`) from the layers of the LPC
character generator, from the drawings of other chapters and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner.

From other chapters, used by path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): Moses (the sheet from
`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), on which the veil below is placed.

The drawings made by `unelte/lpc/cortul_mari.py` (the tabernacle set up, the courtyard, the tabernacle from the inside,
the cloud and the close-up views of the objects) are drawn by us, from code, from our own shapes (`cortul_desen.py`,
`cortul_chivot.py`, `cortul_cort.py`), with nothing from the packs.

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them under the same
terms.
