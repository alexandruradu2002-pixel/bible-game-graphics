# Credits — chapters 31, Gideon, and 29, The Jars (the "lpc" style)

The people, animals and objects of the two chapters are used by path from other chapters (their authors are in their
chapter's credits file): the villagers, the women, the elders and the people (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the men
of Israel, Joash, the prophet, the Midianites (the Amalekites) and the empty jar (`graphics/the-wilderness/`, `credits/the-wilderness.md`),
the boy (Purah) and the father from The Plagues, the stable (`graphics/the-plagues/`, `credits/the-plagues.md`), Gideon
(`graphics/the-spies/`, `credits/the-spies.md`), the angel (`graphics/jacob/`, `credits/jacob.md`), the houses, the oak,
the reeds, the basket, the sack, the folded tent, the altars, the stones, the goats and the villager
(`graphics/the-call/`, `credits/the-call.md`), the big house, the big stone and the fleece (`graphics/peniel/`,
`credits/peniel.md`), the straw and the Hebrew man (`graphics/the-basket/`, `credits/the-basket.md`), the hearth, the flat
stone and the servant's tent (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the olive tree, the rocks, the boulders, the bushes
and the tamarisk (`graphics/moriah/`, `credits/moriah.md`), the small olive tree and the bull (`graphics/the-flood/`,
`credits/the-flood.md`), the vine, the sheaf and the shepherd boy (`graphics/joseph/`, `credits/joseph.md`), the willow and the
angel's light (`graphics/balaam-and-nebo/`, `credits/balaam-and-nebo.md`), the low stone wall (`graphics/the-brothers/`), the fields of grain and
the sack of wheat (`graphics/the-storehouses/`, `credits/the-storehouses.md`), the black tents (`graphics/the-generations/`,
`credits/the-generations.md`), the small sheaf, the smoke and the horn (`graphics/jericho/`, `credits/jericho.md`), the
kneading trough, the unleavened bread, the cooked calf and the calf (`graphics/mamre/`, `credits/mamre.md`), the pot
(`graphics/jacob/`), the jar and the camels (`graphics/the-well/`, `credits/the-well.md`), the fire (`graphics/sinai/`,
`credits/sinai.md`); the ground of the maps from `credits/haran.md`.

Drawn by us, from code (`capitole/ghedeon_desene.gd`): the threshing floor with the wheat, the caves in the hillside, the
views from the stronghold and from the Valley of Jezreel, the camp of Midian seen from above, the fallen black tents, the
torch, the jar with the light and the shards, the flail, the hand bringing water to the mouth and the drops, the man
lying under a cloak, the dream in smoke (the barley loaf) and the glint.
