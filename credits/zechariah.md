# Credits — chapter 62, Zechariah (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/zechariah/` are made by the chapter's tool (`unelte/lpc/zaharia_desene.py`) from the drawings of
other chapters, recoloured (the brighter colours of the New Testament trial: clean clothes, cream-gold stone, blue-violet
shadows) and drawn further by us, in the LPC manner, and from drawings made by us. The drawings made from code by the
chapter (`capitole/zaharia*.gd`: the smoke of the altar and of the incense, the haze in the holy room, the sunbeams with
the specks of dust, the line of the coals, the arrows and the circles of the mini-games) are drawn by us, with nothing
from the packs.
