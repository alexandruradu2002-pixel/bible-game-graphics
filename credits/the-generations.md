# Credits — chapter 4, The Generations (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-generations/` are cut out of or composed from free LPC packs on OpenGameArt.org and from the
LPC character generator, and some of them are drawn by us, in the LPC style. The sounds in `sunete/neamuri/` are
synthesised by us (`unelte/sunete_neamuri.py`), with no third-party files.

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the
Credits screen. Images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground, the patches, the trees, the plants, the fence, the anvil, the furrows, some
of the people's layers) remain CC-BY-SA as well: anyone may reuse them under the same conditions.

## The tools

The images are rebuilt with the tools in the chapter's working folder (`unelte-neamuri/`: `oameni.py`, `animale.py`,
`obiecte.py`, `pete.py`, then `tabele.py`, which writes the tables into `parti/neamuri.gd`), and the preview
(`variante/lpc/previzualizare-neamuri.png`) with `previzualizare.py`. The sounds: `unelte/sunete_neamuri.py`.
