# Credits — chapter 16, The Blessings (the "lpc" style)

The chapter has few drawings of its own. The banners of the twelve sons in `graphics/the-blessings/` are drawn by
us, pixel by pixel, with nothing from the packs (`unelte/lpc/binecuvantarile_steaguri.py`). The threshing floor of Atad and the bier
on the cart are drawn in code, also by us (`capitole/binecuvantarile_desene.gd`). The hill with the cave at Mamre
(`binecuvantarile_deal_a.png`) is drawn by us, pixel by pixel, with nothing from the packs
(`unelte/lpc/binecuvantarile_pestera.py`).
The inside of Israel's tent, from the scene through Joseph's eyes, Israel's hands and Joseph's hand are drawn
in code, also by us (`capitole/binecuvantarile_patul.gd`); the sons seen from behind and Israel sitting are made in the game
from their sheets, which belong to the chapters Joseph and The Storehouses.

The rest of the drawings belong to other chapters, used as they are; their authors are listed in the credits of those chapters. The
ones taken from chapters that are not yet in the store apps (the fire, the smoke, the reed mat) are copied unchanged into
`graphics/the-blessings/` (18 Nov 2026), so that the app does not have to carry the whole packs of those chapters for them:
