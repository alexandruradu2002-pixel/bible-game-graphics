# Credits — chapters 33, Samson, and 31, Delilah (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/samson/` are made by the chapters' tool (`unelte/lpc/samson_foi.gd`) from the people sheets of
other chapters; the gates of Gaza, the pillars of Dagon's house, the winepress, the big mill and the stretcher are drawn by the same tool,
pixel by pixel, with nothing from the packs. The drawings made from code by the chapters (`capitole/samson_poveste.gd`,
`capitole/samson_scuturat.gd`, `capitole/samson_ghicitoare.gd`, `capitole/dalida.gd`: the honeycomb, the swarm of bees,
the ropes and the bowstrings, the fire of the sacrifice, the ring and the riddle cards, the cracks, the beam of the mill, the locks pinned to the
wall, the bar of the gate, the outstretched hand) are drawn by us, with nothing from the packs.

From other chapters, loaded by their path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): the ground and the tents from Haran
(`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the people from The Burning Bush (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), Judah, the foreigners,
the woman, the table, the grain fields, the shade shelter and the cart from The Storehouses (`graphics/the-storehouses/`, `credits/the-storehouses.md`), the house, the trees,
the reeds, the basket, the jug and the Canaanites from The Call (`graphics/the-call/`, `credits/the-call.md`), the houses and the foreigner from
Mamre (`graphics/mamre/`), the oven, the hearth and the firewood from The Generations (`graphics/the-generations/`), the well and the wine jug from
Jacob (`graphics/jacob/`), the large stone, the wall and the small bushes from The Brothers (`graphics/the-brothers/`), the olive trees, the bushes, the rocks,
the boulders and the firewood from Moriah (`graphics/moriah/`), the small olive tree, the trunk and the lioness from The Flood (`graphics/the-flood/`),
the fences, the bread and the boy from The Plagues (`graphics/the-plagues/`), the vine, the sheaf, the basket of loaves and the storage jar from Joseph
(`graphics/joseph/`), the fallen sheaf from Jericho (`graphics/jericho/`), the fire and the hand mill from The Basket
(`graphics/the-basket/`), the rock, the spring, the young goat, the Amalekites and the Israelites from The Wilderness (`graphics/the-wilderness/`), the goat from
The Call; for chapter 34: the city wall and the gate from Mamre, the mat from The Plagues, the willow, the pillar of Baal (the image
of Dagon), the lords, their servants and the barber from Balaam and Nebo (`graphics/balaam-and-nebo/`, `credits/balaam-and-nebo.md`), the jug from The Well
(`graphics/the-well/`), the Gazites and Samson's brothers from The Burning Bush, the ox from The Storehouses, the rock-cut tomb from Gibeon
(drawn from code by `capitole/gavaonul_desene.gd`), each with the credits file of its chapter.

**What the licences require:** the images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them
under the same conditions. The authors of the layers are those in the credits file of the chapter the base sheet is taken from.
