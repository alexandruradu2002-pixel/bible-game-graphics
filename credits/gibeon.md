# Credits — chapter 30, Gibeon (the "lpc" style)

The chapter's people, animals and objects are reused by path from other chapters (their authors are in their
chapter's credits file): Joshua, Caleb and Eleazar (`graphics/the-spies/`, `credits/the-spies.md`), Rahab, the envoys,
the Gibeonites, the houses, the trough, the stones from the Jordan, the reeds, the oaks and Abram's altar (`graphics/the-call/`,
`credits/the-call.md`), the envoys and the Gibeonites (`graphics/the-storehouses/`, `graphics/mamre/`), the people, the elders, the shepherd and the
women (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, `credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the men of Israel, the armies of the Amorites and the donkeys (`graphics/the-wilderness/`,
`credits/the-wilderness.md`), the tents and the hearths (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the well and the boundary pillar
(`graphics/jacob/`), the olive trees, the tamarisks, the rocks, the boulders, the bushes and the woodpile (`graphics/moriah/`), the small olive
tree (`graphics/the-flood/`), the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting, the tent and the bronze altar (`graphics/the-tabernacle/`), the heap of
stones (`graphics/peniel/`), the fields of grain (`graphics/the-storehouses/`), the ark (`graphics/the-tabernacle/`), the folded tent and the jar
(`graphics/the-call/`). The envoys' belongings on the cloth beneath the wall, drawn small, are also reused: the waterskin
(`graphics/the-call/chemarea_burduf.png`), the sandals (`graphics/the-burning-bush/rugul_sandale.png`), the garment
(`graphics/jacob/iacov_haina.png`), the bread (`graphics/the-plagues/urgiile_paine.png`) and the bundle of wood
(`graphics/moriah/moria_lemne_snop.png`), with the authors in their chapter's credits file.
The envoys' belongings in the close-up view (the waterskin, the sandal, the garment, the loaves), the city of Gibeon, the banners of the companies,
the hail, the sky with the sun and the moon, the small map of the south, the cave at Makkedah, the log bridge and the stones of the ford are
drawn by us, in code (`capitole/gavaonul_desene.gd`).
