# Credits — chapter 10 (Moriah)

The drawings in `graphics/moriah/`. The chapter also uses the prologue's drawings (`graphics/haran/`: Abraham, Sarah, the tents,
the well, the hearth), with their authors in `credits/haran.md`. The authors below must appear on the Credits
screen. Images made from CC-BY-SA material remain CC-BY-SA.
