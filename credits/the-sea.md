# Credits — chapter 21, The Sea (the "lpc" style)

The chapter's people, animals and objects are used by path from other chapters (their authors are in their chapter's
credits file): Moses, Aaron, Miriam, the elders, the people and the donkeys (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`,
`credits/the-burning-bush.md`), the Boy and his parents (`graphics/the-plagues/`, `credits/the-plagues.md`), the children
(`graphics/the-basket/`, `credits/the-basket.md`), the old woman (`graphics/the-storehouses/`), the young goat (`graphics/the-call/`,
`credits/the-call.md`), the tents, the hearths, the palm trees, the bushes and the rocks (`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`).
The sea, the walls of water, the sea floor, the pillar, the shadows of the chariots, the wheels and the spears on the shore are drawn by us, from
code. Also by us, from code, are the large obstacles in the run through the sea (the sea boulder and the heap of seaweed, since 6 Nov
2026, `capitole/marea_desene.gd`).

**What the licences require:** images made from CC-BY-SA material (Moses, below) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may
reuse them under the same terms.
