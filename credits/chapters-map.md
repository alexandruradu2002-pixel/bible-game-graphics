# Credits — the "Chapters" screen (the Road: the map, the book, the lineage)

The road map is made from the game's drawings: the ground, the trees and the forest of the style, and the rocks, the tents, the palm trees,
the acacias, the bushes and the well from `graphics/haran/` (the authors in `credits/haran.md` and `credits/shared.md`).

## The paintings in the book

Old paintings, in the public domain, turned into pixels by us (`unelte/tablou_drum.py`: 140 × 160 pixels, 20
colours, close to the game's tones), only a part of each. The public domain also allows them to be sold.
