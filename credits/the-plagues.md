# Credits — chapter 19, The Plagues ("lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/the-plagues/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/urgiile_*.py`) from free LPC packs
on OpenGameArt.org, from the layers of the LPC character generator and from drawings made by us, in the LPC manner.
Moses, Aaron and Miriam are the ones from chapter 18 (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`, see `credits/the-burning-bush.md`); Pharaoh, his servants and
the throne are from chapter 15 (`credits/joseph.md`); the taskmasters, the Hebrews at the bricks, the old man, the donkey, the goats and
the kiln are from chapter 17 (`credits/the-basket.md`); the ox is from chapter 15 (`credits/the-storehouses.md`), the sheep is the
game's own (`credits/shared.md`).

**What the licences require:** the names of the authors below and the links to their pages must appear on the Credits
page. The images made from CC-BY-SA material (the ground of the maps, the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may
reuse them under the same terms.
