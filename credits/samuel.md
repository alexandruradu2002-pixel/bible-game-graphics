# Credits — chapter 37, Samuel (the "lpc" style)

The images in `graphics/samuel/` are made by the chapter's tools (`unelte/lpc/samuel_oameni.gd` and
`unelte/lpc/samuel_foi.gd`): the people's sheets from the sheets of other chapters, recoloured and with new poses, and the objects pixel by
pixel. The drawings made in code by the chapter (`capitole/samuel*.gd`: the waves of the ear and its wheel, the ring around the cart,
the light of the lamps, the bubble of the silent prayer, the white flash of the thunder) are drawn by us, with nothing from the packs.

From other chapters, reused by path (their authors are in their chapter's credits file): the ground and the tents from Haran
(`graphics/haran/`, `credits/haran.md`), the tent, the ark, the altar, the basin, the lampstand, the table, the reed mat, the smoke and
the cloth from The Tabernacle (`graphics/the-tabernacle/`), the pillar, the pots and the tents from Jacob (`graphics/jacob/`), the woodpile, the olive trees,
the bushes, the rocks and the boulders from Moriah (`graphics/moriah/`), the wood from The Generations (`graphics/the-generations/`) and from The Tower
(`graphics/the-tower/`), the sack, the chair, the donkey and the people from The Storehouses (`graphics/the-storehouses/`), the fences, the curtain, the bread,
the bowl, the wheat and the boy from The Plagues (`graphics/the-plagues/`), the fire, the people, the noblemen and the elders from Balaam and Nebo
(`graphics/balaam-and-nebo/`), the embers, the shed, the staff and the reapers' poses from The Threshing Floor (`graphics/the-threshing-floor/`), the table from The Well
(`graphics/the-well/`), the jar, the sack and the waterskin from The Call (`graphics/the-call/`), the wall, the gate, the houses and the cattle from
Mamre (`graphics/mamre/`), the stone pillar from Samson (`graphics/samson/`), the large stone from Peniel (`graphics/peniel/`),
the sheaf from Joseph (`graphics/joseph/`), the stone altar from The Flood (`graphics/the-flood/`), the closed jar and the men of
Amalek from The Wilderness (`graphics/the-wilderness/`), the people from The Burning Bush (`graphics/the-burning-bush/`), the priests from Jericho
(`graphics/jericho/`), the children from The Basket (`graphics/the-basket/`), each with its chapter's credits file.

**What the licences require:** images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them
under the same conditions. The authors of the layers are those in the credits file of the chapter the base sheet is taken from.

## The sounds

The ones in `sunete/samuel/` are computed by `unelte/sunete_samuel.py` (Eli's breathing, the cough, the mouse,
the drip, the oil, the blows on clay and on wood, the wheels of the cart, the snorting of the cows, the fall of Dagon, the note at the
bend, Eli's cry), with no recordings. The rest are reused from other chapters, by name.
