# Credits — chapters 35, Ruth, and 33, The Threshing Floor ("lpc" style)

The people sheets shared by the two chapters, in `graphics/ruth/`, are made by `unelte/lpc/rut_foi.gd` from the people sheets
of other chapters; only the colours of the clothes are changed. What each chapter adds (drawings, people, gestures) is listed
below, under its own chapter.

**What the licences require:** the images made from CC-BY-SA material (the people) remain CC-BY-SA 3.0: anyone may reuse them
under the same terms. The authors of the layers are those in the credits file of the chapter the base sheet is taken from.

## Chapter 35, Ruth

The chapter's drawings: reused from other chapters (the houses, the well, the oven, the mounds, the posts, the olive trees, the terebinth,
the bushes, the wormwood, the rocks, the walls, the city gate, the storage jars, the shade shelter, the fields of grain, the sheaves, the stones of the ford; the list
with the paths is in `parti/rut.gd`, `DIN_ALTELE`), and their authors are in the credits file of the chapter they come from. The new ones:

| Our file | Made from | Modified by us |
|---|---|---|
| `graphics/ruth/rut_rut_gesturi.png` (Ruth gleaning, bowed down to the ground, lying on a mat, winnowing, beating out the ears of grain, sitting at the table) | `graphics/ruth/rut_rut.png` (from `graphics/the-burning-bush/rugul_fata_1.png`, the authors in `credits/the-burning-bush.md`) | Yes: the body bent from the hip (rotated pixel by pixel), the arms, the stick, the cloth and the bread drawn by us; made by `unelte/lpc/rut_gesturi.gd` |
| `graphics/ruth/rut_noemi_gesturi.png` (Naomi with open arms, embracing her daughters-in-law) | `graphics/ruth/rut_noemi.png` (from `graphics/samson/samson_mama_batrana.png`, the authors in `credits/samson.md` and `credits/the-burning-bush.md`) | Yes: the arms moved and drawn by us; made by `unelte/lpc/rut_gesturi.gd` |
| `graphics/ruth/rut_vrabie.png` (the sparrows in the field) | drawn by us, pixel by pixel (since 1 Nov 2026, in place of the recoloured dove from [LPC] Birds) | Not taken from anywhere; made by `unelte/lpc/rut_gesturi.gd` |

The footprints, the ears of grain, the handful of stalks, the stubble, the upper field of grain, the travel bundle, the rolled-up mat, the sickle and the ephah are
drawn by us, from code (`capitole/rut_desene.gd`), with nothing from the packs.
