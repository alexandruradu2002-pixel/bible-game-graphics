# Bible Game: A Pixel Story · pixel art

The pixel art of [Bible Game: A Pixel Story](https://playbiblegame.com), a game that follows the story of the
Bible, published here so that anyone can reuse it, as its licences require. The game's code is not here.

Most drawings start from the free **Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC)** packs on OpenGameArt.org and from the Universal
LPC Spritesheet Character Generator; the rest were drawn by us in the same style (32×32 pixel tiles). For every
file, `credits/` says what it was made from, who drew the source, under which licence, and what we changed.

## Licences

- A drawing made from LPC material keeps the licences of its sources, listed in `credits/` (mostly CC-BY-SA
  3.0/4.0, GPL 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0, CC-BY 3.0/4.0 or CC0). Our changes are released under the same licences.
- Drawings we made from scratch (marked "drawn by us" in `credits/`) are released under
  [CC-BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- If you use them, credit the authors listed in `credits/`, and share drawings you make from share-alike
  material under the same terms.

## What is where

The drawings used in many places (the ground, trees, rocks, Adam and Eve) are at the top of `graphics/`; each
chapter has its own folder, in the order of the game. File names are the ones the game uses internally, in
Romanian (`copac_1.png` is "tree 1"); the credits list every file under that name.
